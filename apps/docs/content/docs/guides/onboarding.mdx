## Overview

Businesses are the top-level entity in Propeller. Every invoice, payment,
and payout belongs to a business. You can register a business directly
(standalone merchant) or nest it under a super-merchant.

## Create a business

<CodeBlock snippets={[
  {
    lang: "curl",
    code: `curl https://api.hyphenmoney.com/v1/businesses \\
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PROPELLER_KEY" \\
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \\
  -H "Idempotency-Key: a-unique-key" \\
  -d '{
    "name": "Acme Exports Ltd",
    "country": "CN",
    "registration_number": "91440101MA5...",
    "settlement_address": "0x..."
  }'`,
  },
  {
    lang: "python",
    code: `resp = requests.post(
    "https://api.hyphenmoney.com/v1/businesses",
    headers={
        "Authorization": f"Bearer {PROPELLER_KEY}",

    },
    json={
        "name": "Acme Exports Ltd",
        "country": "CN",
        "registration_number": "91440101MA5...",
        "settlement_address": "0x...",
    },
)
print(resp.json()["id"], resp.json()["status"])`,
  },
]} />

The response includes a `business_id` and `status` (initially `pending`).

## KYB process

Once created, Propeller automatically initiates KYB via PayKKa:

1. **Registration check** — validates the business registration number.
2. **Director identity** — verifies the legal representative.
3. **UBO check** — identifies ultimate beneficial owners.

Typical turnaround: **2–4 business hours** for Chinese businesses, up to
24 hours for others.

## Settlement setup

Set the `settlement_address` at creation time or update it later:

```bash
curl -X PATCH https://api.hyphenmoney.com/v1/businesses/biz_01J... \\
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PROPELLER_KEY" \\
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \\
  -d '{"settlement_address": "0x..."}'
```

## Checking status

```bash
curl https://api.hyphenmoney.com/v1/businesses/biz_01J... \\
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PROPELLER_KEY"
```

Statuses: `pending` → `active` | `rejected`. Propeller also sends a
webhook (`business.activated`) when KYB completes.
