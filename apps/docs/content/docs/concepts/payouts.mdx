## Overview

Once NGN payments are confirmed and cleared, Propeller executes the FX
conversion and delivers USDC to the merchant's destination. The payout
is a three-legged transfer: NGN debit, FX conversion, USDC credit.

## FX execution

Propeller uses **Globalstack** as the off-ramp provider. The FX rate is
locked at the time of payout initiation (not at payment time). Propeller
quotes a rate, debits NGN from the operational pool, and Globalstack
delivers USDC on the other side.

## Settlement batching

Payouts are **batched daily** to optimize for FX rates and gas costs:

- **Cutoff**: 16:00 WAT (West Africa Time).
- **Execution**: Batches run at 17:00 WAT.
- **Settlement**: USDC arrives at merchant destination within 1–2 hours of execution.

Individual transfers can be tracked in the ledger with full audit trail.

## Destination types

Merchants can settle USDC to:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **EVM address** | Any Ethereum/Polygon/Arbitrum address. |
| **Exchange deposit** | Binance, Bybit, or OKX deposit address. |
| **TRC-20 address** | TRON network USDT (coming soon). |

Propeller does not custody USDC — funds are delivered directly to the
destination you specify at onboarding.
