## Overview

Payments in Propeller are collected via **NIP bank transfers** (NIBSS
Instant Payment). When a business issues an invoice, Propeller creates a
dedicated virtual account. The end-payer transfers NGN to that account,
and Propeller confirms receipt.

## Virtual accounts

Virtual accounts are provisioned through **Paystack**. Each payment request
gets a unique account number. The end-payer sees:

- Bank name
- Account number
- Account name (the merchant's business name)

## Payment flow

1. **Invoice created** → Propeller generates a payment link.
2. **End-payer opens link** → sees payment details (amount, bank, account number).
3. **End-payer transfers NGN** → via their bank app or USSD.
4. **Paystack sends webhook** → Propeller receives `charge.success`.
5. **Propeller confirms** → credits the merchant's ledger account.

## Confirmation

Propeller waits for Paystack's webhook confirmation before crediting the
ledger. The settlement worker polls for confirmations and creates the
corresponding TigerBeetle transfers. End-payers see a confirmation screen
immediately after their bank transfer completes.
