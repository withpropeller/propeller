## Overview

Propeller is **Merchant of Record (MOR)** — the legal counterparty to every
transaction. This means compliance obligations sit with us, not with the
merchant or super-merchant. Propeller handles:

- **KYB** — business verification for every onboarded merchant.
- **KYC** — identity verification for end-payers above NGN 500k.
- **Sanctions screening** — OFAC, FATF, CBN, and EFCC lists.
- **Transaction monitoring** — AML pattern detection.

## KYB — business verification

When a business is registered, Propeller runs KYB through **PayKKa**:

- Business registration number (China: USCC).
- Legal name and registered address.
- Director/UBO identity verification.

Businesses are created in `pending` status and transition to `active` once
KYB clears. You can check status via `GET /v1/businesses/{id}`.

## KYC — payer verification

End-payers are verified through **Dojah** (Nigeria):

- BVN (Bank Verification Number) lookup.
- NIN (National Identity Number) — for transactions above NGN 5M.
- Selfie liveness check for first-time payers.

## Sanctions screening

Every party is screened against:

- **OFAC SDN** list (via ComplyAdvantage).
- **FATF** high-risk jurisdictions.
- **CBN/EFCC** domestic watchlists.
- **Name-match** against the billed party on every invoice.

## Regulatory framework

Propeller operates under Nigerian regulation (CBN, EFCC, NDPC for data
protection) and global standards (FATF, OFAC). Partners PayKKa and
Globalstack carry China-side licensing obligations.
